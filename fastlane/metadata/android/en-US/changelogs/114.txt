Kiyori 1.6.0.1 restores the complete phone experience and removes the retired Wear OS module.

Highlights:
- New default navigation: Home, Anime, Manga, Explore, and Calendar
- Lossless migration for custom tabs and shortcuts
- Responsive Discover actions on phones and wider screens
- Strong biometric or device-credential app lock with timeout support
- Notifications and deep links resume at the correct destination after unlock
- Improved character/team loading, filters, voice-language fallback, and empty states
- Updated typography, ratings, and nested thread comments
- FOSS and GMS universal release builds with hardened signing verification
